# A.P.E.X.: ROVER
**[Challenge: Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars]([url](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/nasas-discarded-equipment-storytelling/))**

  Our project is based on the first challenge, which involves telling stories about NASA missions that were abandoned in space (primarily on Mars). We have developed a game in which the player must locate these lost probes and discover their stories. This is significant because these missions were very important to help us understand more about our universe, yet many people are not even aware that these missions ever existed. Our project addresses this challenge by sharing these stories in a dynamic and engaging way, perfect for those who want to learn more, as well as for those simply looking to have some fun.
  
  Our game is called Rover. The player controls a rover that can go to the Moon and to Mars, the player will face obstacles in these celestial bodies, in the Moon there are some asteroids and in Mars there are some dust devils. Throughout the gameplay the abandoned equipment can be found, when the player interacts with them, a menu will appear telling the story of the discarted equipment and its mission.

  For programming the game we used [Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu/) and for making the sprites we used [Piskel](https://www.piskelapp.com/) and [Paint.NET](https://paint.net/).
